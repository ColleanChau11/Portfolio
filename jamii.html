Marketing at Jamii Esplanade

Events, video, social and web for a Toronto community arts organisation.

Marketing Coordinator · May – Aug 2025 and May – Jun 2026

Jamii Esplanade is a not-for-profit arts organisation in Toronto’s Esplanade community. As Marketing Coordinator, I ran its Facebook and Instagram content, recorded and edited video, designed graphics, and built and maintained pages on its website. The raffle campaign has its own page →

Events

I promoted every event during my terms and recorded and edited the video for each, including:

  • The raffle
  • LAINI
  • WAYO
  • Indigenous Peoples’ Day
  • Joy in the Park (June 26 – 27)
  • PASI

Social media

I posted most of the content on Jamii’s Facebook and Instagram, and my manager posted on some days. On Instagram between May 15 and July 12 (whole account):

  • 96,461 views and 22,018 accounts reached.
  • 48% of views came from people who didn’t already follow Jamii.
  • The top reel had 11K views.
  • Followers grew 2.7%, to 4,208.

Website

  • Built pages for the monthly Cycle dance workshops, the outdoor Joy in the Park events at David Crombie Park, and the online store, where I photographed and edited images of the paintings for the product pages.
  • Kept every page on brand and audited layouts for usability and accessibility.
  • While I supported Jamii’s digital marketing, website sessions rose 25% and unique visitors rose 30%.
