Brand guidelines

One shared place for the logo, colours, fonts and message.

Data on the Spot, about 3–4 weeks

The challenge

The company only had a logo. There was no clear place to get it, people estimated the brand colours so they kept changing, and anyone who needed assets had to ask several people.

What I did

  • Built the guidelines with a marketing colleague and reviewed them with the company president.
  • Wrote the audience, purpose, mission and vision. My colleague did the imagery and brand personality, and my boss wrote the slogan.
  • Kept the two existing colours (logo blue and grey), added the supporting colours, and chose Inter so people stopped using a different font each time.
